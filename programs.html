Our programmes

Opportunity for all.

The activities below describe programme areas and intended initiatives. Availability, eligibility and schedules may vary; contact the Trust for current information.

Home / Our Programmes
♡01 / Women Empowerment

Confidence and financial independence

Programme areas aim to strengthen confidence, decision-making, leadership and livelihood choices for women.

  • Self-Help Group (SHG) formation and strengthening
  • Financial literacy and banking awareness
  • Women's entrepreneurship development
  • Tailoring and handicrafts training
  • Food-processing skills
  • Beauty-care skills training
  • Leadership development
  • Micro-enterprise support
📚02 / Child Development

Learning, protection and growth

Education, nutrition, creativity and child protection are important to equal opportunities for every child.

  • After-school tuition and learning support
  • Scholarships for girls, subject to availability and eligibility
  • Child-rights awareness
  • Digital literacy
  • Nutrition support
  • Creative and extracurricular activities
  • School-attendance improvement initiatives
✚03 / Health and Welfare

Awareness and connections to care

Intended activities focus on informed health choices, prevention awareness and links to qualified providers.

  • Free medical camps, where organised
  • Women's health check-ups
  • Maternal and child health awareness
  • Nutrition awareness
  • Menstrual hygiene education
  • Community health screening
  • Vaccination awareness
↗04 / Livelihood and Financial Inclusion

Skills to income opportunities

Vocational skills, entrepreneurship and financial awareness can help individuals and families work towards sustainable income.

  • Micro-business development support
  • Skill-to-income initiatives
  • Handmade-product development
  • Cooperative production opportunities
  • Digital marketing training
  • E-commerce training
  • Institutional finance facilitation
◉05 / Awareness and Social Campaigns

Rights, safety and participation

Awareness activities aim to promote equality, safety, social responsibility and access to information.

  • Women's rights awareness
  • Domestic-violence awareness
  • Self-defence training for girls
  • Girl-child awareness initiatives
  • Anti-child-labour awareness
  • Community development surveys
✦06 / Training and Capacity Building

Skills for confidence and employability

Training areas are intended to strengthen employability, digital confidence, communication, entrepreneurship and awareness of rights.

  • Computer training for women
  • Spoken English training
  • Digital marketing
  • Entrepreneurship training
  • Vocational and skill-development programmes
  • Legal-aid clinics and counselling support, where available
  • Research and awareness initiatives on women's safety
Programme availability may vary. Activities, batches, services and eligibility are subject to current resources and arrangements. Please contact the Trust to confirm current availability. The descriptions above do not imply that every service is continuously operating.
