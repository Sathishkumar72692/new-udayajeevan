About Udaya Jeevan Trust

Udaya Jeevan Trust is a public charitable trust based in Perambur, Chennai, Tamil Nadu, India. The Trust works towards the social and economic empowerment of women, the development and welfare of children, improved community health, sustainable livelihoods and greater awareness of social rights.

Through community-based initiatives, skill development, financial literacy, educational support, health awareness and partnerships, the Trust seeks to create opportunities for individuals and families to build healthier, more secure and self-reliant lives.

Our approach is rooted in dignity, inclusion, compassion, accountability and community participation. We believe lasting social development is achieved when people have access to knowledge, resources, opportunities and the confidence to shape their own futures.

Our vision

A society where everyone can thrive

A society where women and children are empowered, educated and healthy, and where families can pursue dignity, equality, financial independence and a sustainable future.

Our mission

Opportunity through community action

To promote the welfare and development of women and children through education, health awareness, skills training, livelihood support, financial inclusion and community-based initiatives.
