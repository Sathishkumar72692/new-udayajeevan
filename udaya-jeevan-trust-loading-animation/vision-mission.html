Vision & Mission

Our vision and mission guide our work with people and partners.

A future where every community can thrive.

Our vision is to support inclusive, resilient communities where women can lead, children can develop and families can access meaningful opportunities. Our mission is to work alongside communities through awareness, capacity-building, welfare initiatives and responsible partnerships.

Review and approve the final vision and mission statements with the trust leadership.
