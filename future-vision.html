Future goals / planned targets

A roadmap for 2026–2030

Progress will depend on community priorities, capacity, partnerships and available resources. No progress percentages are asserted.

FUTURE TARGET 01

500+

Support Self-Help Groups

Work towards supporting and strengthening 500+ Self-Help Groups through learning, leadership and peer networks.

FUTURE TARGET 02

10,000

Livelihood support for women

Aim to provide livelihood support to 10,000 women through skills, financial awareness and enterprise pathways.

FUTURE TARGET 03

Expand health outreach

Explore opportunities to expand health outreach programmes across Tamil Nadu with appropriate partners.

FUTURE TARGET 04

Digital education centres

Work towards establishing centres that support digital learning and technology confidence.

FUTURE TARGET 05

Women’s entrepreneurship incubation hubs

Plan hubs that can connect women entrepreneurs with training, mentoring, product development and markets.

FUTURE TARGET 06

Institutional funding partnerships

Strengthen engagement with eligible institutions and explore suitable funding partnerships.
